The model

Separate channels, shared funnel.

The portfolio's engineering backbone is one shared engine behind seven front doors — application layer, permissioned ledger, smart-contract automation, ZK-proof identity — engaged only where each platform genuinely needs it. Marketing follows the same discipline: each initiative earns its own identity — its own YouTube channel, its own content voice, its own social handles — because a wellness audience and an edtech audience don't search, watch, or trust the same way.

But every channel is also a doorway to the others. A parent watching an AI Adaptive Tutor video about exam stress is one end-screen away from a Being & Becoming wellness episode. A viewer learning about a government education scheme on the Setu channel is one link away from an AI Adaptive Tutor scholarship-prep session. The initiatives compete for attention separately, and convert together — with the remaining four platforms (Azad Tours, TofuChain, Audio Books, ONE-NESS Community) folded into the same flywheel as they come to market.

This page is the tracker: what each channel is for, how it feeds the others, and where the numbers get logged.
